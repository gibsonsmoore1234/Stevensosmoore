Student petition

A $30,000 Student Legacy Art Series for Duke Public Spaces.

We are calling on Duke Arts, Duke Student Government (DSG), and Campus Facilities to fully fund and install a 10-piece large-scale painting series created by student artist Gibson Moore. Sign the petition below to show university leadership that Duke students support investing campus funds into permanent, student-created visual art.

The vision

Ten paintings. Permanent homes on campus.

A cohesive series of ten large-scale paintings in varied canvas sizes, made with archival oil and acrylic so they hold their color for decades.

The series is planned for permanent or semi-permanent installation in the places students actually pass every day:

  • Bryan Center
  • Brodhead Center
  • Rubenstein Arts Center

Why this matters to students

Duke's walls should show Duke's own students.

Keep it in the community. Major campus art commissions should go to Duke's own student creators, not be outsourced to outside contractors.

Leave a legacy. Permanent student work tells every future class that what they make here matters enough to hang on the walls.

Spend visibly. A fixed, published budget means students can see exactly what campus funds are buying.
